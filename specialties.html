What We Recruit For

Deep expertise across the roles that shape modern financial services and technology.

Data & Analytics

We help organizations build the leadership and technical capabilities to turn data into better decisions—from enterprise data strategy and governance to engineering, analytics, and business intelligence.

Representative roles

Chief Data Officer; VP or Director of Data & Analytics; Data Governance Leader; Analytics Manager; Data Engineer; Data Analyst; BI Developer.

Artificial Intelligence

We connect organizations with leaders who shape AI strategy and specialists who develop, implement, and support practical AI solutions.

Representative roles

Chief AI Officer; VP or Director of AI; AI/ML Engineering Manager; AI Engineer; Machine Learning Engineer; Data Scientist; AI Implementation Specialist.

Technology & Software

We recruit the leaders and professionals who build, integrate, secure, and support technology—from executive oversight to software delivery, cloud platforms, and infrastructure.

Representative roles

CIO; CTO; VP or Director of Technology; VP or Director of Software Engineering; Engineering Manager; Software Developer; Cloud Engineer; Systems Engineer.

Commercial Lending

We help banks and credit unions hire experienced leaders and producers who grow business relationships, strengthen lending teams, and support sound credit decisions.

Representative roles

Chief Lending Officer; Head of Commercial Banking; VP or Director of Business Lending; Market President; Commercial Lending Manager; Commercial Lender; Commercial Underwriter; Credit Analyst.

Credit Unions

We partner with credit unions to recruit leaders and specialized professionals who understand member service, organizational growth, and the demands of a regulated financial institution.

Representative roles

CEO; CIO; Chief Lending Officer; VP or Director of Digital Banking, Payments, or Risk; Department Manager; Payments Specialist; Compliance Analyst; Lending Professional.
