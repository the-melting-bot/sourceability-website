Let’s Talk

Hi, I’m Tanya.

Founder & President of SourceAbility

I founded SourceAbility because I wanted recruiting to feel more personal and meaningful—for both the organizations hiring and the professionals considering a career move.

Earlier in my career, I worked in a traditional staffing environment where success was often measured by numbers, activity, and volume. While I gained valuable experience, I also saw how easily the people behind those numbers could get lost in the process.

I wanted to step back from that transactional approach and create a company where relationships came first—where I could take the time to understand an organization’s goals, learn what truly matters to a candidate, and remain personally involved throughout the process.

That vision became SourceAbility.

Throughout more than 20 years in recruiting, I have had the opportunity to build lasting relationships with clients and professionals across Data, Technology, and Financial Services. Many of those relationships have continued through multiple hires, career moves, and stages of organizational growth.

One of the most rewarding parts of my work is seeing the difference the right opportunity can make in someone’s life. That may mean helping a professional join an organization where they can thrive, take an important step forward in their career, move into leadership, or significantly increase their earning potential.

It is equally rewarding to reconnect with a client a year or two after a placement and hear how much that person has contributed to the organization. Those are the moments that remind me why this work matters: the right career move can change someone’s future, and the right hire can strengthen an organization for years to come.

That is the kind of experience I wanted SourceAbility to provide when I founded the company, and it continues to guide how I work today.

Why I Do What I Do

“I enjoy working with organizations and individuals where relationships matter, reputations matter, and decisions have a long-term impact.

The best hiring outcomes come from looking beyond a résumé or job description. They require a clear understanding of the organization’s goals, the candidate’s capabilities and motivations, and what long-term success should look like for both.”

What We Value

Integrity

We believe trust is earned through honesty, sound judgment, and doing what is right—even when the conversation is difficult. We represent every client, candidate, and opportunity accurately and professionally.

Partnership

We approach recruiting as a partnership, not a transaction. We take the time to understand each organization’s business, culture, hiring challenges, and long-term objectives before beginning the search.

Transparency

Clear, candid communication is essential to a successful search. We provide honest feedback, realistic market insight, and consistent communication throughout the process.

Quality Over Quantity

We do not measure success by the number of résumés presented. Our focus is identifying and delivering well-qualified candidates whose experience, motivation, leadership capability, and career goals align with the opportunity.

Commitment

We bring a strong work ethic, personal accountability, and persistence to every engagement. When we accept a search, we are committed to representing the opportunity thoughtfully and seeing the process through.

Respect

Every person deserves to be treated fairly, professionally, and with consideration. We respect our clients’ time, protect candidate confidentiality, and recognize the importance of every hiring and career decision.

The SourceAbility Difference

SourceAbility offers the experience and market reach of a long-established search firm with the personal attention and flexibility of a boutique partner.

Our clients work directly with the person leading the search. Our candidates receive honest communication and thoughtful representation. Every engagement is approached with care because our reputation—and yours—is reflected in every interaction.

We strive to be professional but approachable, strategic but practical, and structured while remaining flexible enough to respond to the needs of each search.

Because when relationships matter, how you conduct the search matters too.
